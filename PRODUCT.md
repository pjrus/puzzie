# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS, and local TypeScript data, selected from the user's brief for a database-free MVP.

## Users

People looking for a quick, friendly brain break on a phone, tablet, or laptop. They want to pick a puzzle, solve it, and feel a small sense of progress without creating an account.

## Product Purpose

Puzzie is a consumer puzzle platform for discovering, playing, and completing a variety of short puzzle types. Success means a visitor can start quickly, receive useful feedback, earn points, maintain a daily streak, and return later with local progress intact.

## Positioning

Puzzie combines a wide mix of quick puzzle formats with a lightweight daily habit: pick a puzzle, solve it, earn points, keep your streak going.

## Operating Context

Sessions are short and self-directed. Visitors browse puzzle categories, play individual puzzles or the daily puzzle, use limited hints, and review local statistics. The MVP has no authentication, database, payments, social features, or cloud leaderboards.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The MVP must include playable interactions for number sequence, word scramble, logic, pattern, maths, riddle, connections, word ladder, odd one out, trivia, code breaker, 4×4 sudoku, and quickfire. Puzzle data is strongly typed and local. Progress and statistics are persisted through a small localStorage utility that is safe during server rendering. Difficulty affects complexity, hints, and scoring. Dates use the user's local date.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Puzzie. The tagline is “A little puzzle for your brain.” The voice is playful, clever, modern, friendly, and quick; copy should stay clear and conversational.

## Evidence on Hand

The user brief is the source of truth for product scope, seed-content minimums, interaction requirements, and visual constraints. No existing logo, brand asset, database, or user research is available. Do not invent testimonials, customers, or performance claims.

## Product Principles

- Start with a playable moment.
- Make every puzzle feel understandable before it feels challenging.
- Reward effort immediately with clear feedback and points.
- Keep progress local, visible, and easy to resume.
- Offer variety without making the interface feel busy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, labelled controls, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, touch-friendly targets, clear error and success messages, and reduced-motion support. Do not communicate state through colour alone.
